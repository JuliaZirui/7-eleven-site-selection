Against 139 Berlin stations, Brandenburger Tor pairs high demand with some of the city's thinnest competition, on weekdays and at weekends

1Measured against 139 Berlin stations, not just against each other

Grey dots: inner-Berlin S/U-Bahn stations; bands and dashed lines: each day type's P10 / P90, the ends of the scoring scale. On both day types Brandenburger Tor is in the city's least contested tenth (9.7 / 13.9 competitors per 10,000 people) and Schönhauser Allee in the most crowded tenth (85.9 / 91.4). At weekends Brandenburger Tor's 16,179 people per day exceed the city's P90, because its visitors do not fall away.

Business fit (0–100): demand and competition, each scaled to the P10 / P90 of 139 inner-Berlin S/U stations, weighted 50 / 50; weekday (Wednesday) and weekend / holiday day (Saturday–Sunday average) scored separately and combined 5 : 2. Demand = residents (MSS 2025) + workers (IHK, verified on-site headcounts) + visitors (Wikipedia pageviews, ≈10 visitors per pageview). Competitors = bakeries, cafés, supermarkets, spätis, fast food and fuel shops open that day (OSM + Overture). Hypothetical case; ranks sites for diligence priority, does not forecast revenue.1 / 2
